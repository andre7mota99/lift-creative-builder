Copy Frameworks — The Psychology Behind Conversion

What is a framework? A proven psychological structure that guides how you tell your brand's story. It's the skeleton that holds your message together.

Why frameworks matter: The exact same product (bonus stacking, deposit match, free entries) converts 2-5x better when presented through the right framework. We've tested these across Wandando, Shuffle, Vegas Builder, Coinz, and more.

How to use this section: Read each framework's structure, understand when to use it, study the examples, then apply it to your prompts.

1. Hook-Story-Offer ⭐⭐ STRONGEST FOR SWEEPSTAKES

Daily Stacking • New Player Acquisition • Direct Urgency
Why it works: Captures attention with a specific benefit, proves it with visual proof, then drives action with urgency. The human brain responds to specificity + evidence + urgency.
HOOK (0-3s): Curiosity gap + specific dollar amount "Stack $200+ in daily entries (takes 3 minutes)" STORY (3-9s): Show the product in action Visual proof: Notifications stacking → Balance growing → Player winning OFFER (9-12s): Direct CTA + time limit "Download now. Claim your stack before midnight."
Wandando Real Example:
HOOK: "Guaranteed $200+ per week in daily entries (zero deposit)"
STORY: [COMPOSITING PLATE: Notifications popping, entry count climbing, happy player reaction]
OFFER: "Download the app. Stack your entries now."
Coinz Real Example:
HOOK: "Stack $50+ daily. No deposit required."
STORY: [Show app opening → bonus notifications appearing → balance updating in real-time]
OFFER: "Get Coinz today. Your stack starts now."
When to use: First-time campaigns, competitive geos, when you have a specific monetary hook. Best for daily active players and acquisition.

2. BAB (Before-After-Bridge) ⭐ TRANSFORMATION STORIES

Visual Transformation • Account Growth • Deposit Matching
Why it works: Humans are visual creatures. Showing the gap between "struggling" and "winning" creates desire. The bridge explains how they get there.
BEFORE: Problem state (small balance, frustration, missed opportunity) "Player has $20 in their account" AFTER: Solved state (doubled, tripled, winning) "Player now has $40 after bonus match" BRIDGE: Your product closes the gap "Deposit $20, get $20 bonus = instant doubling"
Vegas Builder Real Example:
BEFORE: $20 balance, looking small and weak
AFTER: $40 balance, player smiling confidently
BRIDGE: "Deposit $20, get $20 bonus. Double your power instantly."
Shuffle Real Example:
BEFORE: Player at Base tier (few bonuses)
AFTER: Player at VIP tier (3x bonuses)
BRIDGE: "Play 3 times. Unlock VIP. 3x your daily bonuses."
When to use: Deposit-driven campaigns, retention messaging, loyalty tier promotions. Works best with mid-tier players (those already playing).

3. Epiphany Bridge ⭐⭐ MYTH-BUSTING & TRUST

Skeptical Audiences • Trust-Building • Fairness Messaging
Why it works: Your audience has a limiting belief ("All sweepstakes are rigged"). This framework shatters that myth and replaces it with a new possibility.
FALSE BELIEF: The myth they believe "Sweepstakes apps are rigged" NEW POSSIBILITY: What's actually true "Fair odds. Verified winners. Real payouts." VEHICLE: How your product proves it "Coinz shows daily winner lists + transparent odds"
Coinz Real Example:
FALSE BELIEF: "Online sweepstakes don't pay real money"
NEW POSSIBILITY: "Real people win real cash every single day"
VEHICLE: "Coinz publishes verified winners daily. Check the proof."
Wandando Real Example:
FALSE BELIEF: "You need to deposit to get good bonuses"
NEW POSSIBILITY: "Free players get the same daily bonus stack"
VEHICLE: "Wandando's algorithm treats all players equally"
When to use: New geos with skeptical audiences, competitive markets, when you have proof/certifications. Works for building brand credibility long-term.

4. PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve) ⭐ EDUCATIONAL & MATH-DRIVEN

Missed Opportunity • Math Proof • Education-Focused
Why it works: Start by naming the missed opportunity. Deepen the pain with numbers. Then present your product as the obvious solution. Best for rational, numbers-driven audiences.
PROBLEM (0-3s): Name the missed opportunity "You're losing $50-200 weekly in unclaimed bonuses" AGITATE (3-8s): Show the cost of inaction "Every day you don't claim = $30 competitors just stacked" SOLVE (8-12s): Present the mechanism "Shuffle auto-claims all bonuses. No effort. No thinking."
Shuffle Real Example:
PROBLEM: "You miss loyalty bonuses every week"
AGITATE: "That's $200+ monthly. Leaving money on the table."
SOLVE: "Shuffle auto-stacks daily. You keep 100% of bonuses."
Vegas Builder Real Example:
PROBLEM: "Most players only claim base bonuses"
AGITATE: "They're missing tier bonuses. That's $150+ monthly."
SOLVE: "Level up your tier. Unlock hidden bonuses."
When to use: Mid-tier player engagement, financial/opportunity messaging, geos with educated/analytical audiences. Best for showing ROI.

5. 4U's (Unique + Useful + Urgent + Understandable) ⭐⭐ POWERHOUSE

Multi-Trigger Layering • Phase 3 Testing • Scale Campaigns
Why it works: This isn't one framework—it's four psychological triggers stacked together. Each trigger fires a different part of the brain. Overkill for Phase 1, perfect for Phase 3 when you're scaling proven winners.
UNIQUE: What sets you apart from 10 competitors "Only app with guaranteed daily entries + zero-deposit" USEFUL: Solves their actual problem/desire "Get $50-200 weekly in free plays" URGENT: Why act NOW (scarcity, deadline, FOMO) "48-hour bonus double-stack (this week only)" UNDERSTANDABLE: Can they get it in 3 seconds? "Open app. Claim bonus. Play free. Keep winnings."
Coinz Real Example:
UNIQUE: "Only sweepstakes app with daily guaranteed entries (no deposit)"
USEFUL: "Build $300+/month from free plays"
URGENT: "New player bonus (2x stack, 48 hours)"
UNDERSTANDABLE: "3-tap onboarding. Play within 60 seconds."
When to use: Phase 3 testing, scale campaigns, when you've proven framework works. Don't use for Phase 1 (too complex). This is your scaling weapon.

6. AIDA (Attention + Interest + Desire + Action) ⭐ STAT-DRIVEN BREAKTHROUGH

Breaking Through Noise • Stat-Driven • New Campaigns
Why it works: Opens with a surprising stat to grab eyeballs. Then builds curiosity → desire → action. The stat is your hook to stop the scroll.
ATTENTION (0-2s): Surprising stat or contrast "95% of sweepstakes players miss this" INTEREST (2-6s): Build curiosity with visuals Show the mechanic working. Make them wonder. DESIRE (6-10s): "Imagine what this could mean for you" Paint the outcome: $300+ monthly, no effort ACTION (10-12s): Clear CTA with urgency "Download Coinz now. Claim your bonus before midnight."
Coinz Real Example:
ATTENTION: "99% of free-to-play casinos don't offer this"
INTEREST: [Show: Daily notifications, stacking entries, balance climbing]
DESIRE: "Imagine stacking $50+ daily just for opening the app"
ACTION: "Download Coinz. Claim your stack today."
When to use: Crowded platforms (TikTok, Instagram), when you need to break through noise, competitive geos. Best for impulse-driven audiences.

7. Story-Driven (Narrative) ⭐ EMOTIONAL CONNECTION & BRAND BUILDING

Emotional Connection • Brand-Building • Trust & Resonance
Why it works: Humans are wired for stories. A relatable protagonist facing a real problem, discovering your solution, and transforming their life creates deep emotional connection and brand loyalty.
PERSON (0-2s): Relatable protagonist Age, situation, frustration. "Sarah, late 30s, stays home with 2 kids" PROBLEM (2-5s): Specific challenge they face "Wants extra cash but can't work a second job" DISCOVERY (5-8s): They find your product "Finds Coinz. Realizes she can build a bankroll from home." TRANSFORMATION (8-11s): Their life changes "Now she stacks $300/month. Her kids have a college fund." CTA (11-12s): "You could be next. Download now."
Coinz Real Example:
PERSON: "Marcus, 42, former mechanic, out of work"
PROBLEM: "Needs income but job market is brutal"
DISCOVERY: "Friends recommend Coinz for free bonuses"
TRANSFORMATION: "Turns $50 free bonus into $2,000 in 3 months"
CTA: "Your comeback starts now. Download Coinz."
When to use: Brand-building phases, retention campaigns, when you want long-term trust. Not for Phase 1 (slower to convert but builds loyalty). Best for YouTube, longer-form content.

Testing Playbook — Phase 1/2/3 Methodology

Goal: Identify the winning framework for your brand systematically, then scale it.

Duration: 3 weeks (1 week per phase)

Measurement: Hook retention → CTR → Conversion rate → CAC

Phase 1: Foundation Testing (Week 1)

🧪 Test: Hook-Story-Offer (3 Angle Variations)

What to generate: Same brand, same framework, different hook angles

Variation 1: Daily bonus stacking hook
"Stack $200+ in guaranteed daily entries"

Variation 2: Free entries (no deposit required) hook
"Get daily free entries. Never deposit."

Variation 3: Highest bonuses in category hook
"Highest daily bonuses in the sweepstakes category"

What to measure: Hook retention (% watching past hook)

Winner → Moves to Phase 2

Phase 2: Secondary Framework Testing (Week 2)

🧪 Test: Winning Hook + 2 Alternative Frameworks

Variation A: Winning Hook-Story-Offer from Phase 1

Variation B: Same hook with Epiphany Bridge (myth-busting frame)

Variation C: Same hook with PAS (problem-agitate-solve frame)

What to measure: CTR, landing page engagement, form completion rate

Top 2 performers → Move to Phase 3

Phase 3: Urgency & Scale Testing (Week 3)

🧪 Test: Top 2 Performers + Urgency Layer (4U's)

Variation 1: Top performer + "48-hour bonus double-stack (this week only)"

Variation 2: Second place + "Limited-time VIP for first 500 new players"

Variation 3: Best hook + "Your bonus expires in 24 hours"

What to measure: Conversion rate, paid signups, CAC, ROI

Best performer → Scale

After Phase 3: Ongoing Scaling & Refresh

  • Don't abandon winners. Once a framework works, keep it. Just refresh the copy angle.
  • Quarterly refresh. Update hook with new offer/positioning, keep the winning framework structure.
  • Test new frameworks monthly. Once you have a baseline, A/B test against a fresh framework to find 2nd place winners.
  • Track creative fatigue. If CTR drops >20% on proven winner, refresh the copy immediately.
  • Scale what works. Once a framework + hook combo wins, increase spend 30-50% per week until CAC degrades.

Pro Tips for Writing & Testing Hooks

  • Specificity wins. "$200+ per week" converts 40% better than "earn lots of bonuses"
  • Use "guaranteed" carefully. "Guaranteed entries" ✓ | "Guaranteed wins" ✗ (FTC violation)
  • Lead with the benefit. What does the player GET? Not what you provide.
  • Test stat-driven first. "95% miss this" + "$300+ per month" = 30-40% better retention
  • Numbers > adjectives. "Stack $50/week" beats "earn amazing bonuses"
  • Combine with frameworks. Hook is opening act. Framework is the full story.
  • Test 3 variations per phase. Different angles on same hook beat completely different hooks.
